PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: 28 August 2026

WorkBee.lk respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of users who access and use our website and services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, how we may share it, and how we protect it when you use WorkBee.lk.

By using WorkBee.lk, registering an account, creating a profile, posting a job vacancy, applying for a job, or otherwise using our services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use WorkBee, we may collect the following information:

1.1 Personal Information

This may include:

  • Full name;
  • Date of birth or age, where required;
  • Gender, where voluntarily provided;
  • Telephone number;
  • Email address;
  • Residential area;
  • Preferred working area;
  • Nationality, where required;
  • Identification information where required for verification;
  • Profile photograph, where provided;
  • Other information voluntarily submitted by you.

1.2 Employment and Professional Information

For workers and job seekers, we may collect:

  • Job skills;
  • Qualifications;
  • Certificates;
  • Work experience;
  • Current employment information;
  • Previous employment information;
  • Preferred job categories;
  • Preferred working locations;
  • Availability;
  • Expected employment information; and
  • Other employment-related information provided by you.

1.3 Employer and Company Information

For employers and companies, we may collect:

  • Company or business name;
  • Business contact information;
  • Contact person's name;
  • Telephone number;
  • Email address;
  • Business address or operating area;
  • Job vacancy information;
  • Job requirements;
  • Company-related information; and
  • Verification information where applicable.

1.4 Technical Information

When you use our website, certain technical information may automatically be collected, including:

  • IP address;
  • Browser type;
  • Device type;
  • Operating system;
  • Website activity;
  • Pages visited;
  • Date and time of access; and
  • Other technical information required for website security and operation.

2. How We Collect Information

We may collect information when you:

  • Register for a WorkBee account;
  • Create or update a profile;
  • Register as a worker or job seeker;
  • Register as an employer or company;
  • Post a job vacancy;
  • Apply for a job;
  • Contact another user through WorkBee;
  • Contact WorkBee;
  • Submit information through forms;
  • Use our website or services; or
  • Otherwise voluntarily provide information to us.

3. How We Use Your Information

WorkBee may use personal information for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts;
  • Providing employment-related services;
  • Connecting workers with employers;
  • Connecting employers with suitable workers;
  • Processing job applications;
  • Displaying relevant worker or employer profiles;
  • Communicating with users;
  • Providing customer support;
  • Verifying information where applicable;
  • Preventing fraud, misuse, and unauthorized activity;
  • Maintaining website security;
  • Improving our website and services;
  • Managing payments and applicable services;
  • Sending important service-related notifications; and
  • Complying with applicable laws and legal requirements.

4. Profile Information and Visibility

WorkBee is designed to facilitate connections between workers and employers.

Depending on the features you use and your account settings, certain information from your profile may be visible to other authorized users.

For example, information such as name, skills, qualifications, work experience, preferred working areas, and employment-related information may be made available to relevant employers or users for employment-related purposes.

We encourage users not to publish sensitive or unnecessary personal information in publicly visible areas of their profiles.

5. Sharing of Information

WorkBee may share information where reasonably necessary to provide our services.

Information may be shared with:

  • Employers or companies when relevant to employment opportunities;
  • Workers or job seekers when relevant to recruitment;
  • Service providers supporting our website or business operations;
  • Payment service providers where applicable;
  • Verification or security service providers where applicable;
  • Government authorities or law enforcement agencies where legally required; and
  • Other parties where you have provided appropriate authorization.

WorkBee does not intend to sell personal information to third parties for unrelated purposes.

6. Job Applications

When you apply for a job through WorkBee, information from your profile or application may be provided to the relevant employer or company.

The employer may use that information to evaluate your application and communicate with you regarding the employment opportunity.

Once information has been provided to an employer, the employer's own privacy practices and policies may also apply.

7. Employer Information

Employers and companies are responsible for using worker information only for legitimate employment and recruitment purposes.

Employers must not:

  • Misuse worker information;
  • Sell or unlawfully distribute worker information;
  • Use worker information for unrelated purposes;
  • Attempt to obtain unnecessary sensitive information; or
  • Use WorkBee information for fraudulent or unlawful activities.

WorkBee may restrict or terminate accounts that misuse personal information.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

WorkBee may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Maintain user sessions;
  • Remember preferences;
  • Improve website functionality;
  • Understand website usage;
  • Improve user experience;
  • Enhance website security; and
  • Analyze website performance.

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

9. Data Security

WorkBee takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, misuse, or loss.

However, no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their passwords and account credentials and should immediately notify WorkBee if they suspect unauthorized access to their account.

10. Data Retention

WorkBee may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide our services;
  • Maintain user accounts;
  • Meet legal or regulatory requirements;
  • Resolve disputes;
  • Prevent fraud and misuse;
  • Maintain business records; or
  • Protect our legal rights.

When information is no longer reasonably required, WorkBee may delete, anonymize, or securely dispose of it in accordance with applicable requirements.

11. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable laws and limitations, you may have the right to:

  • Access personal information held about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate information;
  • Update your account information;
  • Request deletion of certain information;
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • Manage certain profile visibility settings; and
  • Ask questions about how your information is handled.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or where necessary for legitimate business or security purposes.

12. Children's Privacy

WorkBee is intended for individuals who are legally permitted to participate in employment or recruitment activities.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child in circumstances where it should not have been collected, we may take appropriate steps to delete the information.

13. Third-Party Websites and Services

WorkBee may contain links or integrations to third-party websites, applications, payment services, or other platforms.

WorkBee is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party services.

Users should review the privacy policies of third-party services before providing personal information to them.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

WorkBee may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When changes are made, the updated Privacy Policy will be published on WorkBee.lk together with a revised "Last Updated" date.

Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions, requests, complaints, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, please contact us through the contact details provided on WorkBee.lk.
